# Cycle Meme Radar — historical calibration

_Last reviewed: 2026-09-07_

This document captures the historical patterns used to design Cycle Meme Radar V3. It is a calibration reference, not a list of tokens whose future performance is assumed to repeat.

## Scope and a terminology correction

The 2023–2024 meme cycle produced several different paths to extreme returns. WIF, POPCAT, BONK, BODEN and BOME were Solana-native examples. PEPE was Ethereum-native, but it is retained as a cross-chain cultural archetype because its early market-cap trajectory is unusually well documented.

## What the winners actually looked like

### PEPE — viral cultural escape

CoinGecko's 2023 Q2 report measured PEPE from the point its market cap first reached $1M and found a peak return of roughly 1,813x, with peak market cap around $1.8B. The same research notes the appeal of the pre-existing Pepe the Frog image. Later CoinGecko research puts its time to $1B at 19 days.

**Radar lesson:** a pre-existing, instantly legible meme can escape crypto subculture extremely quickly. Exchange listings are accelerants and liquidity events; they should not be treated as the origin of the cultural signal.

Sources:
- https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/2023-q2-crypto-report
- https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/animal-meme-coin-1-billion

### dogwifhat (WIF) — portable native meme

WIF launched in November 2023. By March 6, 2024, The Block reported a ~$2.1B market cap after the image/token had gone viral; CoinGecko's milestone study estimates 81 days to $1B. CoinGecko's Q1 2024 report shows WIF flipping BONK to become Solana's largest memecoin during the quarter.

**Radar lesson:** simplicity and remixability matter. A token that can be repeated, adapted and recognized with almost no explanation can propagate across independent communities. High attention matters, but only when it converts into holders and market depth.

Sources:
- https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2024-03-06-dogwifhat-all-time-high-binance-listing-280799
- https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/animal-meme-coin-1-billion
- https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/2024-q1-crypto-report

### POPCAT — slow-burn survivor

POPCAT demonstrates that launch-week speed is not mandatory. CoinGecko's milestone research estimates 284 days to $1B. In July 2024, The Block documented the token briefly crossing $1B FDV after months of accumulation and a large purchase; CoinDesk reported the broader $1B milestone. The meme itself pre-dated the token and supported continuous user-generated content.

**Radar lesson:** a candidate must not disappear merely because it is quiet for 24 hours. Persistent community, holder retention and survival across months can be more important than initial velocity.

Sources:
- https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/animal-meme-coin-1-billion
- https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2024-07-21-630000-buy-briefly-brings-popcat-over-1-billion-fdv-to-win-polymarket-bet-306514
- https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2024/07/22/popcat-crosses-1b-mog-rallies-as-solana-ethereum-beta-bets-gain-favor

### BONK — chain-community survivor

BONK was launched during a very weak period for Solana. BONK's own history describes more than half the supply being distributed to ecosystem developers and creators. The Block reported that BONK had gained more than 11,000% during 2023 and then fell more than 70% from its December 2023 high. It nevertheless remained a major Solana meme and later recovered.

**Radar lesson:** broad chain-native distribution and survival after a material drawdown are strong evidence. A token that has never experienced stress is `NOT_TESTED`, not resilient by default.

Sources:
- https://www.bonkcoin.com/about
- https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2024-01-08-bonk-falls-solana-memecoin-demand-decrease-270819

### BOME — hypervelocity launch

CoinGecko's Q1 2024 report records BOME launching March 14 and reaching $1B market cap in two days.

**Radar lesson:** extreme velocity is a useful discovery signal but cannot prove durable culture. The engine should mark this pattern as hypervelocity/unproven until holder retention and post-stress survival exist.

Source:
- https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/2024-q1-crypto-report

### BODEN — topical meteor / negative control

BODEN was one of the political meme breakouts of early 2024. CoinMarketCap records an April 8, 2024 ATH around $1.07; by September 2026 the token is roughly 99.9% below that high. CoinGecko's later state-of-memecoins research notes that political meme categories suffered major subsequent declines.

**Radar lesson:** a topical narrative can produce enormous multiples before cultural retention fails. Fast price + attention with weak holder absorption and untested resilience must generate a `meteor risk` warning rather than a durable-cycle promotion.

Sources:
- https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/jeo-boden/
- https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/state-of-memecoins-2025

## Ecosystem-level evidence

CoinGecko reports that the top 10 Solana memecoins gained 801.5%, or $8.32B of aggregate market cap, in Q1 2024. The same report shows multiple viable paths: WIF at ~$4.6B by quarter end, BONK ~$1.9B, BOME ~$802M, POPCAT ~$354M and BODEN ~$239M.

This is important because the detector must not optimize for one launch shape. The winning set contained:
- ultra-fast launches;
- multi-month slow burns;
- chain-native community distributions;
- evergreen internet memes;
- topical narratives that later collapsed.

Source:
- https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/2024-q1-crypto-report

## Social attention: useful but intentionally capped

A 2025 Journal of Banking & Finance study finds abnormal user-generated Twitter attention positively associated with contemporaneous and next-day cryptocurrency returns, with the result driven by user-generated ticker tweets rather than official project tweets. Separate academic work on Twitter promotion and crypto pump-and-dumps also finds that social promotion can generate abnormal price/volume reactions and losses for delayed sellers.

**Radar lesson:** social attention is a short-horizon accelerator and discovery feature. It cannot independently produce Alpha, Smart Money, Safety, holder retention or a durable Cycle Meme classification.

Sources:
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378426625001384
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2024.103479

## V3 feature mapping

Historical observation | V3 feature
---|---
PEPE/WIF cultural escape | independent social scouts + source diversity + culture score
WIF/BOME early acceleration | short-horizon attention velocity, deliberately low weight
POPCAT slow burn | persistent 90-day universe + evidence decay rather than 24h deletion
BONK post-crash survival | material-drawdown resilience state machine
BONK distribution | holder growth + concentration trend
PEPE/WIF adoption conversion | holder growth / retention and holder-vs-price absorption
BODEN meteor risk | price outruns holders + untested resilience warning
Whale / smart-money confirmation | current established Money holdings + historical economic events
Listings/liquidity | market structure / exitability, never treated as cultural origin

## V3 scoring philosophy

Current target weights:
- Culture / independent propagation: 20%
- Holder adoption, retention and distribution: 20%
- Established Money adoption: 15%
- Post-drawdown resilience: 15%
- Market structure / liquidity: 10%
- Short-horizon attention velocity: 8%
- Safety: 7%
- Momentum without pure chase: 5%

Safeguards:
1. Cycle Potential is never Entry Score.
2. Exact Robinhood contract is mandatory.
3. Seeded/pinned candidates receive visibility only, never score.
4. Provisional Money wallets are observation-only until they earn evidence; sample-zero attribution cannot create edge.
5. Current token holding is adoption evidence, not proof of a recent BUY.
6. `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_TESTED` and `TESTING` are not converted to `PASS`.
7. `CHAIN_ICON` / `CYCLE_MEME` require measured holder history and measured post-drawdown resilience with minimum confidence.
8. Rising Fastest is the change in Cycle Potential over 6h/24h/3d, not raw mentions or market cap.
9. Historical analogue labels are explanations only and never add points.
10. A candidate can be a high Cycle Potential token while tactical state remains `NO_RECENT_SIGNAL`, `WATCH`, `DO_NOT_CHASE`, `BLOCKED` or `DISTRIBUTION`.

## Calibration goal for Robinhood Chain

The target is not to notice the next MICRODUCK only after it reaches $20M. The target is to detect when a $1–3M exact-contract token begins to reproduce a high-quality subset of the early evidence seen in durable historical winners: independent cultural spread, holder absorption, market depth, established-money adoption and survival after stress — while explicitly separating that pattern from a BODEN/BOME-style meteor.
